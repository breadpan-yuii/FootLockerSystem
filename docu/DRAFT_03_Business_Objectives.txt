BUSINESS OBJECTIVES

General Objective. To develop a database-driven Laravel web application
that simulates the operations of an online footwear retail business,
providing a customer storefront and an owner/admin management system.

Specific Objectives. The project specifically aims:

 1. To build a public storefront where customers can browse, search,
    and filter products by brand, category, and gender, and sort them
    by price or name.
 2. To let customers view detailed product information, including
    multiple product images, available size and color variants, and
    stock status.
 3. To implement a shopping cart that lets logged-in customers add
    items, update quantities, and remove items before checkout, with
    stock validated on every addition.
 4. To implement a checkout and order system that records delivery
    details, computes the shipping fee automatically (free at a
    subtotal of PHP 3,000 and above, otherwise PHP 150), and creates
    each order with a unique order number (format: FL-XXXXXXXX).
 5. To provide order tracking so any customer can check an order's
    status using its order number, without logging in.
 6. To provide a wishlist in which customers can save products they
    are interested in.
 7. To create a secure owner/admin panel supporting full management
    of products, categories, and brands, plus management of customers
    and orders, including order-status transitions (Pending,
    Confirmed, Preparing, Completed, or Cancelled).
 8. To design a normalized relational database (Third Normal Form) and
    implement it with Laravel migrations and Eloquent relationships,
    protecting data integrity through primary keys, foreign keys,
    unique constraints, and enumerated fields.
 9. To keep every piece of information dynamic - retrieved from and
    stored in the database - so that changes made by the admin appear
    on the customer-facing website immediately.
10. To produce clear documentation, including an ERD, a data
    dictionary, and system diagrams, that accurately describes the
    implemented system.
