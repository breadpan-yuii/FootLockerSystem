SYSTEM FEATURES

The system has two major parts: the customer (public) storefront and
the owner/admin panel. Everything described below reads from and writes
to the database - nothing on the customer-facing site is hard-coded.

A. CUSTOMER / PUBLIC SIDE (Storefront)

1. Home Page. Hero banner, featured products, brand tiles, and category
tiles drawn from the database.
[Figure 1: storefront/05_01_home.png - Home Page]

2. Shop / Product Catalog. Lists all active products with keyword
search and filtering by category, brand, and gender (men, women, kids,
unisex), plus price-range filtering and sorting by price or name.
[Figure 2: storefront/05_02_shop_filters.png - Shop Page with Filters]
[Figure 3: storefront/05_03_search.png - Search Results]

3. Product Detail Page. Product name, brand, description, and price
with sale-price display; image gallery (primary plus additional
images); size and color variant selection with per-variant stock;
stock status with an add-to-cart button; and related products.
[Figure 4: storefront/05_04_product_detail.png - Product Detail Page]

4. Shopping Cart. Add items to the cart, update quantities, and remove
items. Every addition is validated against variant or product stock,
and the page shows the live subtotal, shipping fee, and total.
[Figure 5: storefront/05_05_cart.png - Shopping Cart]

5. Checkout. The customer confirms delivery details (full name, phone,
address, optional notes) and chooses a payment method (Cash on
Delivery, GCash, or Bank Transfer). Placing the order creates it inside
a database transaction with a unique order number (format FL-XXXXXXXX);
shipping is free at a subtotal of PHP 3,000 and above, otherwise
PHP 150.
[Figure 6: storefront/05_06_checkout.png - Checkout Page]
[Figure 7: storefront/05_07_order_confirmation.png - Order Confirmation]

6. Order Tracking. Any visitor can track an order by entering its order
number - no login required - and see its current status.
[Figure 8: storefront/05_08a_track_form.png - Order Tracking Form]
[Figure 9: storefront/05_08b_track_result.png - Order Tracking Result]

7. Customer Account. Registration and login (Laravel Breeze); My Orders
with per-order detail views; a wishlist for saving products; and
profile management (update information, change password, delete
account).
[Figure 10: storefront/05_11a_login.png - Login Page]
[Figure 11: storefront/05_11b_register.png - Registration Page]
[Figure 12: storefront/05_09a_my_orders.png - My Orders]
[Figure 13: storefront/05_09b_order_detail.png - Order Detail View]
[Figure 14: storefront/05_10_wishlist.png - Wishlist]
[Figure 15: storefront/05_12_profile.png - Profile Page]

B. OWNER / ADMIN SIDE (/admin - restricted to the admin role)

1. Dashboard. Store statistics: revenue excluding cancelled orders,
total and pending order counts, product, customer, brand, and category
counts, the eight most recent orders, the top five products by items
ordered, and a per-status order breakdown.
[Figure 16: admin/06_01_dashboard.png - Admin Dashboard]

2. Manage Products (CRUD). Create, edit, and delete products; upload
the primary image and manage gallery images; manage variants (size,
color, SKU, stock).
[Figure 17: admin/06_02_products_list.png - Products - List]
[Figure 18: admin/06_03a_products_create.png - Products - Create Form]
[Figure 19: admin/06_03b_products_edit.png - Products - Edit Form]

3. Manage Categories (CRUD). Create, edit, and delete categories with
name, description, image, gender, and active flag.
[Figure 20: admin/06_04a_categories_list.png - Categories - List]
[Figure 21: admin/06_04b_categories_form.png - Categories - Create/Edit Form]

4. Manage Brands (CRUD). Create, edit, and delete brands with name,
logo, description, and active flag.
[Figure 22: admin/06_05a_brands_list.png - Brands - List]
[Figure 23: admin/06_05b_brands_form.png - Brands - Create/Edit Form]

5. Manage Customers. View the customer list and individual customer
details.
[Figure 24: admin/06_06_customers_list.png - Customers - List]
[Figure 25: admin/06_07_customers_detail.png - Customers - Detail View]

6. Manage Orders. View orders and order details (items, delivery
details, totals) and update order status through Pending, Confirmed,
Preparing, and Completed (or Cancelled).
[Figure 26: admin/06_08_orders_list.png - Orders - List]
[Figure 27: admin/06_09_orders_detail.png - Orders - Detail View]
[Figure 28: admin/06_10_orders_status_update.png - Orders - Status Update]

TECHNOLOGIES USED. Backend: Laravel 13 on PHP 8.3 with Eloquent ORM
and Laravel Breeze authentication. Database: MySQL with migrations and
seeders. Frontend: Blade templates with Tailwind CSS, Alpine.js, and a
Vite build (assets compiled with "npm run build").

DYNAMIC BEHAVIOR. All catalog data (products, categories, brands) is
stored in and retrieved from the database: anything the admin creates
or edits appears on the storefront immediately. Stock and variant stock
control what customers can add to the cart; customer orders appear in
the admin order list for processing; and status changes are reflected
in the customer's My Orders page and the public tracking page.
