02 - BUSINESS / PROJECT OVERVIEW
========================================================================

Business Name : Foot Locker PH (student demonstration store)
Business Type : Online footwear and streetwear retailer (physical products)
Website       : A Laravel-based e-commerce web application

DESCRIPTION
-----------
Foot Locker PH is a web-based online store that sells branded footwear
(sneakers, running shoes, and casual shoes) along with select apparel.
The system simulates the operations of a real footwear retail business:
customers browse a product catalog, filter by brand, category, and gender,
view product details with size/color variants, add items to a shopping
cart, place an order, and track its status. The store owner/administrator
manages the entire business - products, categories, brands, customers, and
orders - through a dedicated admin panel, without editing any source code.

TARGET CUSTOMERS
----------------
- Sneaker enthusiasts and casual buyers looking for branded footwear.
- Men, women, and kids shopping for shoes and apparel.
- Online shoppers who prefer browsing, comparing prices, and ordering
  from home with Cash on Delivery, GCash, or Bank Transfer.

BUSINESS PURPOSE
----------------
- Provide a realistic online shopping experience for footwear products.
- Allow the owner to manage inventory and sales digitally.
- Demonstrate a complete, database-driven Laravel web application that
  covers both the customer (public) side and the owner (admin) side.

SCOPE
-----
- Physical products only (footwear and apparel), no digital goods.
- No real online payment gateway. Payment methods are Cash on Delivery,
  GCash, and Bank Transfer (manual, chosen at checkout),
  and orders move through the statuses:
  Pending -> Confirmed -> Preparing -> Completed (or Cancelled).
- Demonstration/educational use only.

SCOPE DETAIL (verified against the code - every IN item cites its source;
every OUT item was verified absent by searching routes, models, and
controllers on 2026-10-12)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
IN SCOPE (implemented):
  S1. Public catalog - Home, Shop/Search/Filter/Sort, Category/Brand
      pages, Product Details with variants/images/related
      (routes/web.php lines 18-23; HomeController; ShopController).
  S2. Guest self-service - register/login/logout + password reset
      (Laravel Breeze, routes/auth.php); public order tracking by order
      number (OrderController::track; web.php lines 24-25).
  S3. Customer commerce (login required) - cart CRUD, checkout + order
      placement with FL-XXXXXXXX numbers, free shipping at >= PHP 3,000
      else PHP 150, My Orders, wishlist toggle, profile CRUD
      (CartController; CheckoutController; web.php lines 27-45).
  S4. Admin (login + admin role) - dashboard stats, product/category/
      brand CRUD with images/variants, customer view, order view +
      status transitions pending->confirmed->preparing->completed
      (or cancelled) (Admin/* controllers; web.php lines 55-68).
  S5. Data layer - MySQL 3NF schema, 11 entities, seeders for catalog
      and demo accounts (database/migrations; database/seeders).

OUT OF SCOPE (explicitly NOT implemented - listed in §14 as future work):
  O1. Online payment gateway (manual Cash on Delivery / GCash /
      Bank Transfer only - CheckoutController validation).
  O2. Email/SMS notifications (no Mail/Notification code in app/).
  O3. Reviews/ratings, discounts/coupons/promos (no such routes/models).
  O4. Public API (routes/api.php is empty).
  O5. Multi-seller marketplace, digital goods, carrier integration,
      analytics exports.
