========================================================================
02 - BUSINESS / PROJECT OVERVIEW
========================================================================

Business Name : Foot Locker PH (student demonstration store)
Business Type : Online footwear and streetwear retailer (physical products)
Website       : A Laravel-based e-commerce web application

DESCRIPTION
-----------
Foot Locker PH is a web-based online store that sells branded footwear
(sneakers, running shoes, and casual shoes) along with select apparel.
The system simulates the operations of a real footwear retail business:
customers browse a product catalog, filter by brand, category, and gender,
view product details with size/color variants, add items to a shopping
cart, place an order, and track its status. The store owner/administrator
manages the entire business - products, categories, brands, customers, and
orders - through a dedicated admin panel, without editing any source code.

TARGET CUSTOMERS
----------------
- Sneaker enthusiasts and casual buyers looking for branded footwear.
- Men, women, and kids shopping for shoes and apparel.
- Online shoppers who prefer browsing, comparing prices, and ordering
  from home with Cash on Delivery.

BUSINESS PURPOSE
----------------
- Provide a realistic online shopping experience for footwear products.
- Allow the owner to manage inventory and sales digitally.
- Demonstrate a complete, database-driven Laravel web application that
  covers both the customer (public) side and the owner (admin) side.

SCOPE
-----
- Physical products only (footwear and apparel), no digital goods.
- No real online payment gateway. Payment is Cash on Delivery / manual,
  and orders move through the statuses:
  Pending -> Confirmed -> Preparing -> Completed (or Cancelled).
- Demonstration/educational use only.

DISCLAIMER
----------
This is an educational student project. It is NOT affiliated with,
endorsed by, or connected to Foot Locker, Inc. or any of the brands
shown. Product names and images are used for demonstration only.
