========================================================================
04 - SYSTEM FEATURES
========================================================================
(Insert screenshots beside the relevant feature. See 05 and 06 for the
screenshot list.)

The system has two major parts: the CUSTOMER (public) side and the
OWNER/ADMIN side.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. CUSTOMER / PUBLIC SIDE (Storefront)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Home Page
   - Hero banner, featured products, brand tiles, and category tiles.
   [Screenshot: Home page]

2. Shop / Product Catalog
   - Lists all active products.
   - Search by product name/keyword.
   - Filter by category, brand, and gender (men/women/kids/unisex).
   - Filter by price range and sort by price/name.
   [Screenshot: Shop page with filters]

3. Product Detail Page
   - Product name, brand, description, price (with sale price display).
   - Image gallery (primary + additional images).
   - Size/color variant selection with per-variant stock.
   - Stock status and add-to-cart button.
   - Related products.
   [Screenshot: Product detail page]

4. Shopping Cart
   - Add items to cart, update quantity, remove items.
   - Live subtotal, shipping fee, and total computation.
   [Screenshot: Cart page]

5. Checkout
   - Enter/confirm delivery details (full name, phone, address).
   - Choose payment method (Cash on Delivery by default).
   - Add optional order notes.
   - Place order -> creates an order with a unique order number.
   [Screenshot: Checkout page]

6. Order Tracking
   - Track an order by entering the order number (no login required).
   [Screenshot: Track order form / result]

7. Customer Account
   - Registration and login (Laravel Breeze).
   - My Orders: list of the customer's orders and their statuses.
   - Order detail view.
   - Wishlist: add/remove saved products.
   - Profile management (update info, change password, delete account).
   [Screenshot: My Orders, Wishlist, Profile]

------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. OWNER / ADMIN SIDE (`/admin`, restricted to admin role)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Dashboard
   - Store statistics: totals for products, categories, brands,
     customers, orders, and sales/order summaries.
   [Screenshot: Admin dashboard]

2. Manage Products (CRUD)
   - Create/edit/delete products.
   - Upload product image; manage product images.
   - Manage product variants (size, color, SKU, stock).
   [Screenshot: Product list + create/edit form]

3. Manage Categories (CRUD)
   - Create/edit/delete categories with image and gender.
   [Screenshot: Categories list + form]

4. Manage Brands (CRUD)
   - Create/edit/delete brands with logo and description.
   [Screenshot: Brands list + form]

5. Manage Customers
   - View customer list and individual customer details.
   [Screenshot: Customer list + detail]

6. Manage Orders
   - View orders, view order details, and update order status
     (Pending, Confirmed, Preparing, Completed, Cancelled).
   [Screenshot: Order list + detail + status update]

------------------------------------------------------------------------
DYNAMIC BEHAVIOR (Rubric Criterion 3)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- All catalog data (products, categories, brands) is stored in and
  retrieved from the database, not hard-coded.
- Any product/category/brand created or edited by the admin immediately
  appears on the storefront.
- Product stock and variant stock affect availability shown to customers.
- Orders placed by customers appear in the admin orders list for
  processing, and status changes are reflected in the customer's
  "My Orders" and the public order-tracking page.
