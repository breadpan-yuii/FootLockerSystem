========================================================================
03 - BUSINESS OBJECTIVES
========================================================================

GENERAL OBJECTIVE
-----------------
To develop a database-driven Laravel web application that simulates the
operations of an online footwear retail business, providing a customer
storefront and an owner/admin management system.

SPECIFIC OBJECTIVES
-------------------
1. To build a public storefront where customers can browse, search, and
   filter products by brand, category, and gender, and sort by price.

2. To allow customers to view detailed product information, including
   multiple product images, available sizes/colors (variants), and stock
   status.

3. To implement a shopping cart that lets logged-in customers add,
   update the quantity of, and remove items before checkout.

4. To implement a checkout and order system that records customer
   delivery details, computes shipping fees, and creates an order with a
   unique order number.

5. To provide order tracking so customers can check the status of their
   order using the order number.

6. To provide a wishlist feature that lets customers save products they
   are interested in.

7. To create a secure owner/admin panel that supports full CRUD for
   products, categories, and brands, plus management of customers and
   orders (including updating order status).

8. To design a normalized relational database and implement it using
   Laravel migrations and Eloquent relationships, ensuring data integrity
   through primary keys, foreign keys, and constraints.

9. To ensure that information is retrieved from and stored in the
   database (dynamic), so that changes made by the admin are immediately
   reflected on the customer-facing website.

10. To produce clear documentation - including an ERD, data dictionary,
    and system diagrams - that accurately describes the implemented
    system.
========================================================================
