BUSINESS / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Business Name : Foot Locker PH (student demonstration store)
Business Type : Online footwear and streetwear retailer (physical products)
Website       : A Laravel-based e-commerce web application

Foot Locker PH is a web-based online store that sells branded footwear
(sneakers, running shoes, and casual shoes) along with select apparel.
The system simulates the operations of a real footwear retail business:
customers browse a product catalog, filter it by brand, category, and
gender, view product details with size and color variants, add items to
a shopping cart, place an order, and track its status. The store owner
administers the entire business - products, categories, brands,
customers, and orders - through a dedicated admin panel, without editing
any source code.

TARGET CUSTOMERS. Sneaker enthusiasts and casual buyers looking for
branded footwear; men, women, and kids shopping for shoes and apparel;
and online shoppers who prefer browsing, comparing prices, and ordering
from home with Cash on Delivery, GCash, or Bank Transfer.

BUSINESS PURPOSE. The project provides a realistic online shopping
experience for footwear products, lets the owner manage inventory and
sales digitally, and demonstrates a complete database-driven Laravel
web application covering both the customer (public) side and the owner
(admin) side.

SCOPE. In scope: the public catalog (Home, Shop, Search, Product
Details); guest self-service (registration, login, public order
tracking); customer commerce (cart, checkout, orders, wishlist,
profile); the admin panel (dashboard, products, categories, brands,
customers, orders); and the MySQL database with seeders. Out of scope:
an online payment gateway (payment is manual - Cash on Delivery, GCash,
or Bank Transfer, chosen at checkout); email or SMS notifications;
reviews, ratings, discounts, and coupons; a public API; and digital
goods. Orders move through Pending, Confirmed, Preparing, and Completed
(or Cancelled). This is a demonstration for educational use only.

DISCLAIMER. This is an educational student project. It is NOT
affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Foot Locker, Inc. or any
of the brands shown. Product names and images are used for
demonstration only.
